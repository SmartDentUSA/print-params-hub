- Professional profile (CoursesProfessionalProfile) shows CRM person/company/deals/NF-e from the canonical lead and stores contract-source checkboxes in prof_contract_sources; the AI history summary runs only on demand via prof-history-summary (staff-only) to control cost.
- Event type (`smartops_events.event_type`: feira_congresso, presencial_parceiros, campanha_promocional) chooses the social AI narrative through `src/lib/eventTypes.ts`; promotional campaigns never mention venue or stand, so one event table serves congresses and offer periods.
- Event card seller breakdown (`fn_event_lead_stats`): promotional campaigns credit the owner of the CRM deal opened for that submission (fallback: current CRM lead owner), while fairs keep the booth consultant; campaigns have no booth, and lead-level consultant snapshots from other events would misattribute.
- Professional card "Leads de cursos" reads `fn_professional_course_lead_stats` (staff-only): canonical leads from immutable `inscricao_curso_publica`/`inscricao_lista_espera` activity of smartops courses where the professional is instructor, recommended or owns the imported copy, crediting deals won after the first enrollment; shown apart from KOL forms and never feeds commissions.
- Partner portal Performance reads the same course-lead rule through the service-only `fn_professional_course_lead_stats_portal` (called by `professional-course-portal` after token validation, with period filter: leads by first enrollment date, sales by won date); the staff-only function stays guarded by auth.uid().
- `CampaignLinkPicker` takes an optional `sections` list (`src/lib/social/groupLinkSections.ts`); the social post "link abaixo da imagem nos grupos" field passes `GROUP_LINK_SECTIONS = ["simulator"]` so Post Grupos only ever offers WhatsApp simulator short links, never form or landing-page ones.
- Marketing e-mail campaigns accept as button only an official s.smartdent.com.br short link of a published landing page, the product form or a WhatsApp simulator (`smart-ops-send-gmail/cta.ts`, options from `src/lib/emailCtaOptions.ts`); every href is forced to it with `?o=email`, so e-mails never link to wa.me or invented URLs. The product is optional when the main button is a published landing page (`canGenerateEmail`); uploaded `custom_html` landings feed the AI their own visible text and first image (prices stripped, `smart-ops-generate-email-ai/custom-landing.ts`) because their structured `content` may be a stale template.
- Catalog product pickers and product filters (form editor product of interest, capture buttons and hero image, course tags, e-mail campaign, WhatsApp flow content, Copilot social, document/video product filters) list only `PRODUCT_CATALOG_ENTITY_TYPES` active rows; the form editor uses the searchable `forms/CatalogProductCombobox` built by `src/lib/catalogProductOptions.ts`, which keeps already-saved links visible as "fora do catálogo", because system_a_catalog also stores testimonial clients, category configs and company data.
- Form settings editors that save whole sets of `smartops_forms` columns (SDR/campaign editor, the form list "editar" dialog, FeiraEventoConfig) open a fresh DB row, save only when `updated_at` still matches (else refuse and ask to reload) and warn about unsaved changes; list objects can be stale and a stale save silently wipes newly added buttons or links.
- Landing-page short links (`smartops_short_links`, target `landing_page`) are created by a DB trigger when a form landing page is published (reusing `generate_short_link`); form cards show the LP chip only for published pages, so every published LP has its s.smartdent.com.br link without a manual click.
- Uploaded custom landing pages live in `smartops_form_landing_pages.custom_html` (images as CDN assets with absolute admin URLs, source copy in `docs/landing-pages/`) and render full-screen in a srcdoc frame (`CustomLandingFrame`) that forwards tracking and `sd-lp-whatsapp` clicks to the page; each `data-wa` item maps through `custom_simulators` (catalog `product_catalog_id` or form `button`) to the WhatsApp simulator, which lia-capture validates server-side as `commercial_preset` and counts per `attendance_channel_events.simulator_id`, so uploaded pages keep CRM capture, exact product names and per-simulator metrics instead of linking to wa.me; every WhatsApp button gets its own `data-slug` (general buttons as `geral-<place>`, with no product so the lead still picks in the buttons) and retired card ids stay in `custom_simulators` so old clicks keep their label.
- Bio landing cards prefer the landing's presentation-only `bio_cover` (countdown phases parsed by `src/lib/lp/countdownCover.ts`, rendered client-side every second) over the static image; phase dates live with the landing so the cover never drifts from the page.
- Click origin (e-mail, SMS, WhatsApp, groups, Bio) travels as `?o=<origin>` on official short links (`src/lib/clickOrigin.ts`, mirrored in `supabase/functions/_shared/click-origin.ts` and the `ace_public_insert` policy); short-link-resolve forwards it as utm_source to landing/form pages and utm_term to the simulator, the landing passes utm_term to its form and simulator, and every `attendance_channel_events` row stores `origin_channel`, read by `fn_attendance_origin_metrics`; links without `o` keep their old destination so existing posts keep working.
- Commercial campaign phrases after conversion (chat confirmation and the seller WhatsApp link) name the validated choice via `chosenItemLabel`: the landing card label from published `custom_simulators` when the lead came from that card, else the button's exact product, else the category; CRM still receives the button/catalog product, so the wording never changes routing.
- Campaign "Segmentar Leads" E-commerce section stores `ecom_*` keys in the same lead_filter/segment object and applies them through `src/lib/campaignEcommerceFilters.ts`, mirrored byte-for-byte in `supabase/functions/_shared/campaign-ecommerce-filters.ts` for server-side e-mail audiences (test enforces equality); purchased products come from `lia_attendances.lojaintegrada_produtos_comprados`, kept by statement triggers on Loja Integrada orders/items (cancelled orders excluded, merged leads roll up to the canonical), because order tables are service-only and PostgREST cannot filter leads through them.
- Every commercial campaign form offers one WhatsApp simulator per active commercial catalog product, built on demand by `src/lib/productSimulators.ts` (`pid` + `sim=p-<catalog id>`, same form so the same conversion origin) and shown in the form card dialog and in `CampaignLinkPicker`; nothing is stored per product, lia-capture validates the catalog product server-side, and the channel comparison of commercial forms lists every catalog product simulator (idle ones collapsed) labeled with the catalog name, so new catalog products get simulators automatically.
- Event card conversion (`fn_event_lead_stats`) uses imported `event_sales` only when the event has rows there (counts wrapped in NULLIF(...,0)); otherwise leads, deals and value all come from CRM deals won after the submission, so the counts never show 0 next to a CRM value.
