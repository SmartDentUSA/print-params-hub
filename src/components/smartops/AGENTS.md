- Professional profile (CoursesProfessionalProfile) shows CRM person/company/deals/NF-e from the canonical lead and stores contract-source checkboxes in prof_contract_sources; the AI history summary runs only on demand via prof-history-summary (staff-only) to control cost.
- Event type (`smartops_events.event_type`: feira_congresso, presencial_parceiros, campanha_promocional) chooses the social AI narrative through `src/lib/eventTypes.ts`; promotional campaigns never mention venue or stand, so one event table serves congresses and offer periods.
- Event card seller breakdown (`fn_event_lead_stats`): promotional campaigns credit the owner of the CRM deal opened for that submission (fallback: current CRM lead owner), while fairs keep the booth consultant; campaigns have no booth, and lead-level consultant snapshots from other events would misattribute.
- Professional card "Leads de cursos" reads `fn_professional_course_lead_stats` (staff-only): canonical leads from immutable `inscricao_curso_publica`/`inscricao_lista_espera` activity of smartops courses where the professional is instructor, recommended or owns the imported copy, crediting deals won after the first enrollment; shown apart from KOL forms and never feeds commissions.
- Partner portal Performance reads the same course-lead rule through the service-only `fn_professional_course_lead_stats_portal` (called by `professional-course-portal` after token validation, with period filter: leads by first enrollment date, sales by won date); the staff-only function stays guarded by auth.uid().
- `CampaignLinkPicker` takes an optional `sections` list (`src/lib/social/groupLinkSections.ts`); the social post "link abaixo da imagem nos grupos" field passes `GROUP_LINK_SECTIONS = ["simulator"]` so Post Grupos only ever offers WhatsApp simulator short links, never form or landing-page ones.
