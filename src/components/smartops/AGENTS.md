- Professional profile (CoursesProfessionalProfile) shows CRM person/company/deals/NF-e from the canonical lead and stores contract-source checkboxes in prof_contract_sources; the AI history summary runs only on demand via prof-history-summary (staff-only) to control cost.
- Event type (`smartops_events.event_type`: feira_congresso, presencial_parceiros, campanha_promocional) chooses the social AI narrative through `src/lib/eventTypes.ts`; promotional campaigns never mention venue or stand, so one event table serves congresses and offer periods.
- Event card seller breakdown (`fn_event_lead_stats`): promotional campaigns credit the owner of the CRM deal opened for that submission (fallback: current CRM lead owner), while fairs keep the booth consultant; campaigns have no booth, and lead-level consultant snapshots from other events would misattribute.
- Professional card "Leads de cursos" reads `fn_professional_course_lead_stats` (staff-only): canonical leads from immutable `inscricao_curso_publica`/`inscricao_lista_espera` activity of smartops courses where the professional is instructor, recommended or owns the imported copy, crediting deals won after the first enrollment; shown apart from KOL forms and never feeds commissions.
- Partner portal Performance reads the same course-lead rule through the service-only `fn_professional_course_lead_stats_portal` (called by `professional-course-portal` after token validation, with period filter: leads by first enrollment date, sales by won date); the staff-only function stays guarded by auth.uid().
- `CampaignLinkPicker` takes an optional `sections` list (`src/lib/social/groupLinkSections.ts`); the social post "link abaixo da imagem nos grupos" field passes `GROUP_LINK_SECTIONS = ["simulator"]` so Post Grupos only ever offers WhatsApp simulator short links, never form or landing-page ones.
- Marketing e-mail campaigns accept as button only an official s.smartdent.com.br short link of the product form or of a WhatsApp simulator (`smart-ops-send-gmail/cta.ts`, options from `src/lib/emailCtaOptions.ts`); every href is forced to it, so e-mails never link to wa.me or invented URLs.
- Catalog product pickers and product filters (form editor product of interest, capture buttons and hero image, course tags, e-mail campaign, WhatsApp flow content, Copilot social, document/video product filters) list only `PRODUCT_CATALOG_ENTITY_TYPES` active rows; the form editor uses the searchable `forms/CatalogProductCombobox` built by `src/lib/catalogProductOptions.ts`, which keeps already-saved links visible as "fora do catálogo", because system_a_catalog also stores testimonial clients, category configs and company data.
- Form settings editors that save whole sets of `smartops_forms` columns (SDR/campaign editor, the form list "editar" dialog, FeiraEventoConfig) open a fresh DB row, save only when `updated_at` still matches (else refuse and ask to reload) and warn about unsaved changes; list objects can be stale and a stale save silently wipes newly added buttons or links.
- Landing-page short links (`smartops_short_links`, target `landing_page`) are created by a DB trigger when a form landing page is published (reusing `generate_short_link`); form cards show the LP chip only for published pages, so every published LP has its s.smartdent.com.br link without a manual click.
- Uploaded custom landing pages live in `smartops_form_landing_pages.custom_html` (images as CDN assets with absolute admin URLs, source copy in `docs/landing-pages/`) and render full-screen in a srcdoc frame (`CustomLandingFrame`) that forwards tracking and `sd-lp-whatsapp` clicks to the page; each `data-wa` item maps through `custom_simulators` (catalog `product_catalog_id` or form `button`) to the WhatsApp simulator, which lia-capture validates server-side as `commercial_preset` and counts per `attendance_channel_events.simulator_id`, so uploaded pages keep CRM capture, exact product names and per-simulator metrics instead of linking to wa.me.
